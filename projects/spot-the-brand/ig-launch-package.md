## 6. Hashtag strategy

Use 3-5 hashtags per post. The mix:

**Always**:
- `#brandmonitoring`
- `#martech`
- `#marketingnl`

**Rotate**:
- `#computervision` (tech audience)
- `#socialmedia` (broad)
- `#agencylife` (peer)
- `#brandnl` (local)
- `#influencermarketing` (adjacent, contested)

**Never**:
- Competitor names (#storyclash, #brand24)
- Trend hashtags unless directly relevant
- More than 5 (IG penalizes spam)
