# Spot the Brand · Social campaign v0.2

A 6-week launch campaign. The brand carries the story. Customer examples rotate in as proof, never as the headline.

> Read [`manifesto.md`](manifesto.md) and [`brandbook.md`](brandbook.md) before producing.

## 1. The campaign idea

> **"What your social tool can't see."**

Every piece of content shows the same act: a visible brand mention in a real social post that legacy tools missed. The audience watches us find it. The bounding box snaps. The confidence number ticks up. The headline lands: *this was invisible to your monitoring*.

We do this for many brands across the campaign. Some are customers (with permission). Some are illustrative (publicly visible content used as commentary, careful with rights). Some are anonymized.

The protagonist of every post is **Spot the Brand**, not the brand we detected.

## 4. Format mix

### Detection-drop reels (9:16, 6-10 sec)
**Always brand-led, customer-rotating.**

Structure:
```
[0.0-0.5s]  Black frame, faint scan-line crossing
[0.5-2.0s]  Real social post fills the frame
[2.0-2.5s]  Red crosshair animates in, settles on product
[2.5-3.0s]  Dashed bounding box snaps. SFX: "blip"
[3.0-4.5s]  Mono label "DETECTED · [category] · 0.94"
[4.5-6.0s]  Creator handle + followers fades in
[6.0-7.0s]  Cut to wordmark: "Spot the Brand. See what your tool misses."
```

Rotation (named customers come only after explicit Named opt-in — see brand-examples.md):
- Week 1: anonymized detection drop — clean tier-1 creator moment with caption "no hashtag, no tag, found anyway"
- Week 2: anonymized again, different angle (festival vs grocery vs lifestyle)
- Week 3: case-study slot using our pilot brand's numbers, categorical framing
- Week 4: another anonymized example from a different category
- Week 5: a tier-1 NL creator moment we've found
- Week 6: hand-picked best-of compilation

The point: the FORMAT is the brand. The CONTENT inside the format rotates. We don't tie our marketing identity to any single customer.

## 6. Detection-drop template — production

Built once in After Effects (or CapCut for the lightweight version). Lukas + studio team owns this.

Components:
- Black frame with faint scan-line texture (loop-able)
- Crosshair animation (snaps in 0.5s)
- Bounding-box snap with "blip" SFX
- Mono text type-on animation for "DETECTED · [text] · 0.XX"
- Creator handle + follower count card
- Wordmark out

Variants needed:
- 9:16 (Instagram Reel + TikTok)
- 1:1 (Instagram feed)
- 16:9 (LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts)

## 8. Hashtag strategy

3-5 hashtags per post max. Mix broad + niche.

- `#brandmonitoring` (broad)
- `#computervision` (niche tech audience)
- `#martech` (niche)
- `#brandnl` `#marketingnl` (local)
- `#agencylife` (peer)

Avoid:
- competitor names
- generic trending hashtags
- hashtags tied to one customer (don't use #stelz on our own posts, even when content features them — keeps the brand at the center)

## 9. Voice in captions

Captions follow the brandbook voice. Always:

- 2-4 short sentences
- Lead with the insight, not the product
- One concrete number or example
- One CTA at the end

Sample caption (week 1 Monday reel):
> Most brand monitoring tools read words. Hashtags. @mentions. But the visible brand moment in this post had neither. Your tool would have missed it. We didn't.
>
> Spot the Brand. Visual brand monitoring for brands that show up in 2026.
>
> Try it free at spotyourbrand.com

Sample caption (week 3 Monday carousel):
> One of our first customers — a Dutch hard seltzer brand — ran us for 90 days. They went from tracking 60 mentions a month to tracking 436. 86% of the new ones had no hashtag.
>
> This is one customer's story. The pattern applies to any brand with a visually distinctive product.
>
> Try it on yours.

Note how STELZ appears as "one of our first customers" — descriptive, not constitutive.

## 13. Ethics & permissions

- **Creator content**: never post a creator's content as our marketing without permission. We work to default-opt-in via our existing customer relationships.
- **Customer content**: STELZ specifically green-lit the use of their dashboard + select detections as marketing examples. Any other customer requires explicit sign-off before featuring.
- **Anonymization**: when we don't have permission, anonymize. Show the bounding-box, but obscure faces and brand identifiers in the content.
- **Competitors**: don't out them in social. We sell against them via the gap, not via attack.

## 15. The discipline

The simplest test for whether a post belongs in this campaign:

> Does this post communicate something about **Spot the Brand**, or about **one of our customers**?

If the answer is "about a customer", rewrite it until the answer is "about Spot the Brand, using a customer to prove the point".

We are a brand. STELZ is a customer. Hold the line.
