Frequently asked.

How is this different from social listening tools?

Traditional listening monitors text — hashtags, keywords, mentions. We monitor pixels. Computer vision scans every image and video frame for your specific product. Result: we find your brand in posts that mention nothing of you, but show your can in hand.

How quickly can you onboard a new brand?

5 minutes for the setup wizard. We scan your last 12 Instagram posts to suggest reference images and a hashtag pool. First detections land within 30 minutes. A populated dashboard within a few days.

How accurate is the detection?

Two-pass detection: fast initial scan with a vision LLM, then strict verification with a higher-capability model. In our STËLZ pilot, this combination removed 33% of false positives from the first pass. Confidence scores per detection so you can tune your own threshold.

What about historical data?

Standard plans look back 7 days. Pro plans 90 days. Enterprise can backfill up to 1 year of historical content. Backfill is a one-time credit-based action that builds your dataset before live monitoring takes over.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the cloud. You get a dashboard URL, automated reports in your inbox, and (Pro+) alerts in Slack or email. No engineering needed on your side.

What if a creator gets archived but I want them back?

Auto-archiving is reversible. Every archived creator is one click from re-promotion. Manual promotions never get auto-demoted. You stay in control.

How does pricing scale with multi-brand groups?

Enterprise pricing is per-brand-equivalent. A holding with 5 brands typically pays less per brand than 5 separate Pro accounts. Contact sales for a setup that fits.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days, full feature access, 100 credits included. No credit card required to start.
